Property 01 · Families that pull

Attraction

What pulls me toward the person.

Sub-properties

  • Physical, beauty standard
  • Charm, charisma, presence
  • Sexual, desire
  • Fetish and specific preferences
  • Intellectual (their mind attracts me)
  • Chemistry (voice, smell, manner, timing; the inexplicable)
  • Status, power, what they represent socially
  • Aesthetic (I like looking, without desire), distinct from sexual

Note

The asexual community separates sexual, romantic, sensual, aesthetic, platonic and intellectual attraction. That cut is better than common sense and fits directly as sub-properties. The status sub-property deserves a distinction: admiring what the person is (Admiration) is not the same as wanting what they give you socially (social capital). The two come mixed, and many people are attracted by the second without knowing it; the Weight of status slider, in Self Discover, measures that.

Where it comes from

The same value can have different roots. For Attraction, the most common:

  • Real attraction, of body and chemistry
  • Novelty (the person is new, and novelty pulls)
  • Status they lend me
  • Prohibition (what I can't have attracts me more)
  • Resemblance to someone from the past

How it shows

Aggressive expression

Says it, shows it, moves forward

Peaceful expression

Keeps it, watches from afar, waits to be noticed

The trait doesn't change the value; it changes what the other sees. Peacefulness is not absence: it is the same value without noise.
