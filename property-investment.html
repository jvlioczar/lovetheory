Property 11 · Families that sustain

Investment

What I spend on them.

Sub-properties

  • Time
  • Money
  • Emotional energy
  • Priority (who do they come before?)
  • Opportunity cost (what I stop living for them)
  • Sacrifice (what I gave up)

Note

High Investment with low Reciprocity is one of the combinations the scale highlights: giving a lot to someone who gives little back.

Where it comes from

The same value can have different roots. For Investment, the most common:

  • Love
  • Sunk cost (I've invested too much to leave)
  • Guilt and duty
  • Fear of being alone
  • Pride in not giving up

How it shows

Aggressive expression

Gives and announces it, demands a return

Peaceful expression

Gives in silence and keeps score inside

The trait doesn't change the value; it changes what the other sees. Peacefulness is not absence: it is the same value without noise.
