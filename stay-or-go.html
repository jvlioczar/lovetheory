Stay or go?

Should I stay with this person?

A test to weigh, not to decide for you. First the red lines, which do not enter the balance. Then what weighs in favor and what weighs against, each item with its score. At the end, a table where you set how much each thing weighs for you, and the reasons you stay, sorted into reasons of love and reasons of prison.
