Keep the Spark

What will we do to keep love good?

Time wears novelty out, and novelty is what feeds passion. What keeps a love good and hot is hardly ever luck: it is what the two of you do on purpose. Here are 188 ideas in three parts: things to do together, new things in bed between the two of you, and experiences that go beyond the couple. Each of you answers alone, without seeing the other's answers, and the match shows only what both want. What one of you does not want is shown to nobody.

How it works

1.

Each answers alone

You choose which parts to open and mark each idea: not for me, maybe, I want this, or we already do and I want to keep it. Skip whatever you like.

2.

The link goes to your partner

Your answers travel inside the link, with no server and no account. Your partner opens it, answers without seeing yours and sends the link back.

3.

Only what matches shows up

The result shows what both want, what is already yours and what deserves a conversation. What either of you declined stays out, and nobody has to explain.

Beyond the couple

Experiences that involve other people or other settings. For some couples this is fuel; for others, it is the end. Nothing here is a recommendation. Only open this part if both of you are willing to look, and agree beforehand what each answer means.

Watching and being watched · Flirting and fantasy with others · With more people · Open agreements

  • There is no right answer, and no idea here is a required step of a good love. Answer by what you want, not by what you think your partner expects.
  • A yes given to please is a no. Maybe means a conversation, not a promise.
  • The part beyond the couple only makes sense if both truly want it. Insisting after a no is pressure, and pressure is a red flag.
