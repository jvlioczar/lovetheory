Property 09 · Families that sustain

Support

What they do for me and I for them.

Sub-properties

  • Emotional
  • Practical (they show up when I need them)
  • Financial, material
  • Availability (they have time for me)

Note

Every Support item has two directions: I give and I receive. The difference between the two feeds Reciprocity.

Where it comes from

The same value can have different roots. For Support, the most common:

  • Affection
  • Duty
  • Bargaining chip (I give to collect later)
  • Guilt
  • Fear of being dispensable

How it shows

Aggressive expression

Solves it for them, takes control

Peaceful expression

Stays close, accompanies, waits to be asked

The trait doesn't change the value; it changes what the other sees. Peacefulness is not absence: it is the same value without noise.

Flags that predict this property

  • green Treats well those who can give them nothing · false when it is a performance for an audience
  • green Gives space without disappearing · false when the space is disinterest
