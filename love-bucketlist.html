Love Bucketlist

The love I want to live

A bucket list is the list of what you want to live before life goes by. This is the one for love, in two parts: the relationship you want to have, with what you want to find in someone and offer back, and the things to live as a pair, from small gestures to big milestones. There are 93 items. Mark what you have lived, what you want to live and what is not for you. What stays marked as wanted is your list.

Loading…

How it works

1.

You mark item by item

Not for me, maybe, want to live it, or living it or lived it. There is no right answer, and nobody's list has to match yours.

2.

Your list is kept

The answers stay on your device only. Come back whenever you like to cross off what you lived and add what you have come to want.

3.

As a pair, if you want

Send the link to your partner. The match shows what both want to live; what one of you does not want stays out.
