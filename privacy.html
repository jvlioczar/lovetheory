Privacy

Privacy policy

The Love Theory does not collect, store or receive your answers. This page says, precisely, what happens to each piece of data.

What you answer

Everything you answer in the tools (Love Scale, portraits, Couple Test, Limits, Keep the Spark, Love Bucketlist, Sexual Bucketlist, Self Love, Self Discover, Stay or Go, My flags) is processed in your browser and stored in your device's local storage. None of it is sent to a server of ours, because we have no server that receives answers.

These answers may include sensitive data, such as sexual orientation, sex life and relationship details. That is exactly why the site was built never to receive them.

The Couple Test and Limits links

To compare answers as a pair, the site puts the answers inside the link itself, after the # sign. That part of the address is not sent to the server when the page opens.

Whoever has the link has the answers. Send it only to the right person, and remember that messaging apps keep the links you send.

How to delete

Use the delete button in My portraits, or clear this site's data in your browser settings. There is no copy on our side to delete.

What the page loads from third parties

Hosting: the site is served by GitHub Pages, which, like any server, logs the IP address of visitors, for security and operation.

Fonts: the fonts come from Google Fonts; when the page opens, your browser requests the fonts from Google, which receives your IP address.

BiblioVerse bar: the bar at the top is loaded from biblioverse.org, another site of the same initiative, and collects no personal data.

Statistics

The site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies, without identifying people and without tracking you across sites.

Cookies

The site uses no cookies.

Who it is for

The content is about relationships and sexuality and was made for adults.

The site is not diagnosis, therapy or professional advice. In situations of violence or risk, use the help lines listed in Stay or Go or your country's emergency services.

Who we are and changes
