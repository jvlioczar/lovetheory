Property 05 · Families that warm

Intimacy

How well we know each other.

Sub-properties

  • Mutual knowledge (I know how they work)
  • Shared secrets
  • Identification, mirror (I see myself in them)
  • Shared history
  • Non-sexual physical intimacy (sleeping together, comfortable silence)

Note

Intimacy is the stock of what is already known; Openness is the channel open now. You can have old intimacy with a closed channel (long marriages, siblings) and an open channel with no intimacy (a stranger on a plane).

Where it comes from

The same value can have different roots. For Intimacy, the most common:

  • Years of living together
  • A crisis gone through together (accelerated intimacy)
  • Identification (the person is like me)
  • Imagined intimacy (I know them by what they show, not by what they are)

How it shows

Aggressive expression

Tells everything, asks everything

Peaceful expression

Knows through daily life and silence

The trait doesn't change the value; it changes what the other sees. Peacefulness is not absence: it is the same value without noise.
