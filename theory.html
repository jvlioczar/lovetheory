The theory

Everything that makes up a love

Love is one word for a mixture of different things. Here the mixture is opened into eighteen properties, each with its parts, its roots and its way of showing.

The model

Property, value, portrait, variability, and where the image we form of someone comes from.

Mechanisms

Where each property comes from and how it shows: root, expression trait, behavior. Jealousy as the model case.

Modulators

Who feels: attachment, personality, trauma, moment, neediness, gender role.

What time does

Why passion falls, what rises, and what sustains the wish to be together.

Red flags and green flags

The heuristics of love: the shortcuts we use, tied to the properties they predict, and when they fail.

Types of love

Greek, Sternberg, Lee; by relationship, by structure, by phase.

Sexuality and attraction

Sexual and romantic orientation, types of attraction, gender role, libido, Fisher's three systems.

Reference theories

What has already been said, where the Love Theory agrees and where it expands.

Glossary

All the site's terms and the related ones, one line each.

The eighteen properties

Grouped by what they do in the bond. Each is an independent axis: the value of one does not pull another, and zero is a legitimate value.
