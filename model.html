The model: property and value

A property is what is felt. A value is the measure of what is felt, at that moment. The rest of the site follows from that.

Rules of the model

Everything changes.

The value is the perception of that moment. That is why the portrait is dated, and why it is worth redoing later: three months, a year.

The properties are independent.

Each is its own axis; the value of one does not pull another. Admiration 9 with Affection 2 and Affection 9 with Admiration 2 are two different loves, not a right one and a wrong one. The site never adds the values into a total.

Zero is a legitimate value.

Friendship has Attraction 0 and is still love. There is no complete love as a goal.

Every value has a variability.

Some people are always at the same point; others swing. The word is variability, not inconsistency: inconsistency judges, variability describes.

Shadows count.

A profile without shadows is a greeting card, not a portrait.

Value and weight.

In the deep scale, besides how much you feel, there is how much it matters to you. The difference between the two is one of the most revealing results: you feel little admiration and that matters a lot to you is a diagnosis in one line.

Where the image comes from

The value you mark is not born only of what you feel; it is born of the image you have formed of the person, and that image has sources with different reliability.

Direct perception

What you saw and lived with them. The main source and the slowest to form.

Expectation

What you expected to feel or find before you knew them. High expectation is the raw material of idealization.

Positive discoveries

What raised the value over time: I didn't expect them to be so loyal.

Negative discoveries

What lowered it: I found out they lie.

Reputation

What people say about them. Weighs on Trust and Admiration before any evidence; the biggest source of borrowed flags.

Approval of the circle

What people say about the two of you. Predicts duration better than common sense admits: the Romeo and Juliet effect did not hold up; network approval predicts stability.

A high value made only of expectation and reputation is a borrowed value; a high value made of perception and discoveries is one's own. The deep scale asks, in each family, whether the person was better or worse than you expected.
