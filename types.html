Types of love

Regions of the portrait space

A type is a region of the portrait space: a recognizable pattern of high and low properties. The site shows each type with its standard profile for you to compare with the portrait you generated. No type is a goal; Sternberg's consummate love, with everything high, is rare and unstable.

The types and their standard profiles

The values are the ones that define the type; the other properties stay free. The Love Scale compares your portrait with each of these.

By type of relationship

Romantic

What common sense calls love. Any combination of properties can be here.

Friendship

Aristotle separated three: of utility, of pleasure, of virtue. They map onto Support, Compatibility and Admiration.

Family

Parental, filial, fraternal, chosen. Storge: Affection, Commitment and long time, often without admiration and without choice.

Fan and parasocial

High Admiration, Passion and Investment, zero Reciprocity, imagined intimacy. McCutcheon's scale has three levels: entertainment, intense-personal, borderline.

Platonic and queerplatonic

High Intimacy and Commitment without romantic or sexual attraction.

Mentor and mentee

High Admiration with Support and Trust.

Colleagues and business partners

Trust and Compatibility of project, with variable Affection.

Ex

Residual love: high time and nostalgia, zero Commitment, shadows in between.

Companion animal

Very high Affection, Support and Trust, zero Attraction, one-sided Openness. Full love by the model, and the proof that the model does not depend on verbal reciprocity.

By structure

Monogamy

Agreed and, in general, desired exclusivity.

Ethical non-monogamy, open relationship, polyamory, relationship anarchy

A different reading of Possession and agreed Exclusivity. Compersion (joy at a partner's joy with someone else) is the inverse of jealousy.

Situationship, friends with benefits

Commitment undefined by choice. The Couple Test shows that the assumed layer tends to diverge.

By phase

Passion or limerence, honeymoon, companionship, long duration, decline, return, grief.
