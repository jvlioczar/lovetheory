Property 08 · Families that sustain

Trust

How safe I feel.

Sub-properties

  • Honesty (they don't lie to me)
  • Loyalty, fidelity (they don't trade me in)
  • Competence (I trust what they do)
  • Predictability (I know what to expect)
  • Emotional safety (I can be myself without punishment)
  • Physical safety

Note

Trust is the property that depends most on reputation before evidence: what people say about the person weighs before any fact. The deep scale asks where the value comes from.

Where it comes from

The same value can have different roots. For Trust, the most common:

  • Their actual behavior
  • Past betrayal by someone else (I distrust in advance)
  • Generalized distrust (I distrust everyone)
  • Projection of my own disloyalty

How it shows

Aggressive expression

Demands proof, interrogates

Peaceful expression

Watches in silence, checks in secret

The trait doesn't change the value; it changes what the other sees. Peacefulness is not absence: it is the same value without noise.

Flags that predict this property

  • green Says I don't know without shame · false rarely false
  • red Promises and fails on the small things · false in a moment of crisis or grief
  • red Makes you feel crazy for feeling what you feel · false almost never false
