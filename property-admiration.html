Property 03 · Families that pull

Admiration

What elevates me in the person.

Sub-properties

  • Character and values
  • Talent, competence, intelligence
  • Achievements, trajectory
  • Aesthetics, style, taste
  • Courage, attitude
  • Respect (I recognize their autonomy, limits, equality)
  • Pride (in them; and in being seen with them)
  • Inspiration (they make me want to be better)

Note

Admiration is the property most often confused with love as a whole and the one that splits most on the inside: you can admire the character and not the competence, the talent and not the values. And you can love without admiring anything: much family love and much long love work that way. That's why the deep scale measures the parts separately.

Where it comes from

The same value can have different roots. For Admiration, the most common:

  • Real merit
  • Idealization (Passion disguised as admiration)
  • Status the person lends me
  • Compensation (I admire in them what I lack)
  • Reputation (what people say, before I saw for myself)

How it shows

Aggressive expression

Praises in public, defends, imitates

Peaceful expression

Admires in silence; sometimes doesn't even say

The trait doesn't change the value; it changes what the other sees. Peacefulness is not absence: it is the same value without noise.
