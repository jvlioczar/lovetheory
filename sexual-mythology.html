Sexual Mythology

What do you actually know about sex?

Hardly anyone had real sex education. We learned from friends, from porn and by improvising, and what is left is a mythology: ideas everyone repeats and that are wrong. Here are 165 statements across twelve themes. For each one, you say whether it is a myth or the truth, and the explanation appears right away. The point of view is declared: sexual freedom, yes, and with responsibility. Without consent, without talking and without caring for whoever is with you, it is not freedom. There are two versions: the short one, with the 42 most important statements, takes about 7 minutes; the full one, with all 165, about 28 minutes.

How it works

1.

Myth or truth

You read the statement and answer: truth, myth or I don't know. I don't know is an honest answer, and it is worth more than guessing.

2.

The explanation comes right away

As soon as you answer, you see whether you got it right and why. Where research or law is behind it, the text says which.

3.

The result is a map

At the end, your correct answers by theme and the list of myths you believed. It stays on your device only.

When it is about other people

What you do when it is not about you: someone runs down your partner, someone is exposed, a friend crosses the line.

  • Getting things wrong here is no shame: it is the expected result of never having been taught. The test is for learning, not for grading a person.
  • People are different: some more reserved, some more open, and both ways are legitimate. Nobody needs to be pressured into anything, and nobody needs to control what others do with their own bodies, as long as they harm no one.
  • There are degrees of wrong. Sex without consent is violence; touching someone who does not want it is assault or harassment; insisting, pressuring and ignoring the other's pleasure is disrespect. None of these is the same thing, and all of them matter.
  • Laws change from country to country. Check the law where you live.
  • If any statement touches a hard experience, stop whenever you want. Seeking help is strength: the channels are on the Stay or Go page.
