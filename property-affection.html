Property 04 · Families that warm

Affection

The warmth.

Sub-properties

  • Tenderness, fondness
  • Wanting to care
  • Missing them, longing
  • Comfort in their presence (I rest near them)
  • Non-sexual touch
  • Compassion, empathy (I suffer with them)

Note

Affection is the property that rises over time when the bond survives the fall of passion. It's what stays.

Where it comes from

The same value can have different roots. For Affection, the most common:

  • Real, built affection
  • Habit and familiarity (the comfort of the known)
  • Pity (downward compassion)
  • Gratitude
  • Duty (I care because I must)

How it shows

Aggressive expression

Hugs, cares in front of everyone, solves things

Peaceful expression

Cares in silence, in the details, without announcing

The trait doesn't change the value; it changes what the other sees. Peacefulness is not absence: it is the same value without noise.

Pointed inward

Self Love

Am I gentle with myself when I fail?
