The theory of love

Look at who you keep close.

That's you standing there.

We say who we are through the people we keep close. Each one is there because something in us is fed by them, and we almost never know how to name what. The Love Theory opens the word love into parts, measures each part, and returns a portrait of what you feel for someone today.

We love and admire in different ways

You can love someone without admiring them. You can admire a person's character but not their competence, or their talent but not their values. You can trust without attraction, feel attraction without trust, invest heavily in someone who gives little back. None of these combinations is wrong: each is a way of loving, and almost nobody can name their own. The properties are independent, and the site never adds the values into a total.
