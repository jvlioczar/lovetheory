Where the Love Theory differs

1

One ruler

For friendship, romance, family and fandom. The other theories measure romance and treat the rest as a minor case.

2

The constellation

Nobody measures the set of people kept close to extract the pattern of the one measuring.

3

The four layers

I feel, I think you feel, agreed, assumed. Existing couple tests measure satisfaction, not the divergence of implicit contracts.

4

Heuristics tied to the scale

Each flag points to the properties it predicts and says when it is false.

5

Shadows in the profile

Not in a separate test.

6

Dated portrait

Never your love type is X.

7

Trait against practice

Self Discover says how the person thinks they work; the constellation shows how they actually work.
