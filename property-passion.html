Property 02 · Families that pull

Passion

How much the person occupies my mental space.

Sub-properties

  • Frequency of thought
  • Euphoria in their presence, withdrawal in their absence
  • Idealization (I see more than there is)
  • Urgency (I need to see them soon)
  • Curiosity, fascination (I want to know everything)
  • Obsession (limerence)

Note

Passion is the property time knocks down hardest: the intense phase lasts 12 to 30 months in most studies. That isn't love ending; it's passion giving way to the rest. See the page on time.

Where it comes from

The same value can have different roots. For Passion, the most common:

  • Attraction plus novelty
  • Limerence (a void of mine filled by the person)
  • Intermittence (the person vanishes and returns, and that is addictive)
  • Prohibition
  • Neediness (hunger for a bond pointed at someone)

How it shows

Aggressive expression

Declares, insists, shows up unannounced

Peaceful expression

Thinks about it all the time and says nothing; follows from afar

The trait doesn't change the value; it changes what the other sees. Peacefulness is not absence: it is the same value without noise.
