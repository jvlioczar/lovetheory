Modulators

Who feels

Every value passes through the filter of the person answering. The site explains the modulators here, measures the main ones in Self Discover and measures the state-based one in the thermometer before each scale.

ModulatorHow it distorts the valueBasis
Attachment styleAnxious raises Possession, Dependence and Passion; avoidant lowers Openness, Intimacy and declared Dependence; secure reads values closest to real.Bowlby, Ainsworth; adults: Hazan and Shaver, ECR scale
PersonalityNeuroticism raises Possession and Shadows; extraversion raises Compatibility and the need for contact; openness to experience raises tolerance for novelty and lowers tolerance for monotony. The basal sliders of Self Discover measure this.Big Five
Gender roleThe script assigned to each gender decides who should provide, care, initiate, yield, protect and show emotion. It inflates or lowers Support, Protection and emotional Openness; men in the expected role mark lower outbound Openness.Social role theory (Eagly)
Aggressiveness and peacefulnessDoes not change how much is felt, changes how it comes out: the aggressive confronts, forbids, attacks the third party; the peaceful goes quiet, avoids conflict, stores hurt. It is the main expression trait.Big Five (agreeableness); Buss and Perry
Experiences and traumaPast betrayal lowers Trust and raises Possession; abandonment raises Dependence; abuse confuses Affection with Fear.clinical
Life momentGrief, stress, moving, hormonal phase alter everything temporarily. That is why the portrait is dated.
Availability for connectionThe state, not the trait: how traumatized or open the person is right now. Closed, it lowers Openness, Trust and Passion for everyone measured. The thermometer before each scale measures this.Window of tolerance (Siegel)
NeedinessHunger for a bond: not lack of contact (that is loneliness), lack of being cared for. Inflates Dependence, Passion, Investment and Possession; lowers standards. It is the opposite pole to closedness on the thermometer.Anxious attachment; emotional deprivation
Culture and religionDefine what can be called love, what one is allowed to feel, what is shameful.anthropology
Reference modelsHow the parents loved (or didn't) becomes the mold of normal.
Self-esteem and self-loveLow self-love inflates Dependence and negative Reciprocity (they can't possibly like me). It is the bridge to Self Love.Neff, Rosenberg
Orientation and sexualityDefines which sub-properties of Attraction exist for the person.
Relational valuesMonogamy and non-monogamy change the reading of Possession and Exclusivity.
NeurodivergenceChanges the reading of signals, the need for predictability, the form of intimacy.
In every result, a short note: people with anxious attachment tend to mark Possession higher than the bond justifies. If you recognize yourself, read your 7 as a 5.
