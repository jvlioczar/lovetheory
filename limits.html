Limits

What counts as betrayal?

Many of a relationship's rules were never said: each one assumes by default what is allowed and what isn't. And what is betrayal for one isn't for the other. Here you answer a hundred situations, alone, and find the map of your limits. Then you send the link to your partner, who answers without seeing yours; the crossing shows exactly where the limits diverge, from the least to the most controversial. That is what needs to be agreed, not deduced.
