Property 15 · Families that grip

Freedom

How much I remain myself inside the bond.

Sub-properties

  • To be myself (my humor, my manner, my personality, without shrinking)
  • Social (I can talk to and see other people)
  • Sexual and affective (I can have other people, when that is what I want and what was agreed)
  • Of life choices (work, place, time, money)
  • Of opinion (I can disagree without punishment)
  • To leave (I could go without catastrophe; staying is a choice)

Note

Freedom is the received side of Possession: what the other's Possession feels like from the inside. Low Freedom is I feel trapped, and feeling trapped can be several things at once: trapped as a person, trapped socially, trapped sexually, trapped in life, trapped by not being able to leave. Not all of it comes from the other: one can trap oneself with guilt, duty or dependence. In the Couple Test, my Freedom crosses directly with the other's Possession.

Where it comes from

The same value can have different roots. For Freedom, the most common:

  • Real freedom
  • Control by the other (their Possession)
  • My own guilt or duty (I trap myself)
  • Dependence (I can't leave, so I feel trapped)
  • Cultural script (that's what a relationship is)
  • Fear of being alone

How it shows

Aggressive expression

Complains, fights for space, threatens to leave

Peaceful expression

Shrinks, pretends everything is fine, leaves on the inside

The trait doesn't change the value; it changes what the other sees. Peacefulness is not absence: it is the same value without noise.

Flags that predict this property

  • green Gives space without disappearing · false when the space is disinterest
  • red Decides for you to protect you · false in a real and immediate risk
  • green Disagrees with you without punishing or shutting down · false rarely false
