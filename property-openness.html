Property 06 · Families that warm

Openness

The channel between us, in both directions.

Sub-properties

  • Outbound, emotional openness: I say what I feel; I show myself vulnerable; I can talk about anything, including conflict; what I hide
  • Inbound, emotional receptivity: I listen without defending myself; I accept what they feel for me; I don't minimize what they bring; I let it change me
  • Repair: after a fight, the channel reopens

Note

The two directions run separately. There are people who say everything and receive nothing (monologue) and people who receive everything and say nothing (confessional). In the Couple Test, the gap between what I feel and what you think I feel is almost always born of low outbound; the gap between what you say and what I register is born of low inbound.

Where it comes from

The same value can have different roots. For Openness, the most common:

  • A genuinely open channel
  • Avoidant attachment (I close by default)
  • Shame
  • I was punished before for opening up
  • Culture (men don't talk)
  • Accumulated contempt that closed the channel

How it shows

Aggressive expression

Direct conflict, sometimes too much

Peaceful expression

Avoids conflict; the channel closes to keep the peace

The trait doesn't change the value; it changes what the other sees. Peacefulness is not absence: it is the same value without noise.
