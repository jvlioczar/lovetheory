Property 10 · Families that sustain

Protection

I place myself between them and what threatens them.

Sub-properties

  • Physical defense (I step in front)
  • Public alliance (I defend them when people talk, even when they're not there)
  • Defense of their reputation, their name, their story
  • Watchfulness (I keep an eye on what could hurt them before it happens)
  • Removing risks (I get them out of situations, people, places)
  • Receiving protection (they do this for me)

Note

Support is giving them something; Protection is standing between them and a threat. The shadow of Protection is overprotection, which turns into control and becomes Possession: the line between I protect you and I decide for you is the point where the protected person stops being consulted.

Where it comes from

The same value can have different roots. For Protection, the most common:

  • Real care
  • Property (I defend what's mine)
  • Projection of my own vulnerability
  • Need to be needed
  • Control disguised as watchfulness

How it shows

Aggressive expression

Attacks the third party, fights for them

Peaceful expression

Mediates, shields, removes them from the scene without conflict

The trait doesn't change the value; it changes what the other sees. Peacefulness is not absence: it is the same value without noise.
