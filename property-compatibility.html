Property 07 · Families that warm

Compatibility

How well our lives fit together.

Sub-properties

  • Of values: moral and ethical, religious and spiritual, political
  • Of practical life: hygiene (cleanliness, order, care of body and home), money, rhythm and energy, lifestyle (food, alcohol, children, where to live)
  • Intimate: sexual (desire, frequency, repertoire; the fit, not the attraction)
  • Of daily life together: humor, interests, lightness, communication and conflict style
  • Of project: the future each one wants

Note

Compatibility is not Admiration. To admire is to look up; compatibility is fit, and it depends on both. You can admire someone's faith without sharing it. Some sub-properties are binary, not scales: children, religion, what one eats. We call those cut-off lines.

Where it comes from

The same value can have different roots. For Compatibility, the most common:

  • Real fit
  • Similarity (we are alike)
  • Complementarity (we are different and it fits)
  • Adaptation (one of us molded to the other)
  • Denial (we pretend it fits)

How it shows

Aggressive expression

Negotiates, discusses the differences

Peaceful expression

Yields in silence; the differences turn into hurt

The trait doesn't change the value; it changes what the other sees. Peacefulness is not absence: it is the same value without noise.

Pointed inward

Self Love

Do I enjoy my own company? Do I live the way I believe?
