Sexual Bucketlist

What I have lived and what I want to live

The bucket list for sex: 165 items across practices, positions, rhythms, roles, occasions and places. Mark what you have lived, what you want to live and what is not for you. It is for knowing yourself, for having something to talk about and, as a pair, for finding out what both want without anyone having to suggest it first. Everything here assumes adults and the consent of everyone involved. There are two versions: the short one, with the 44 essential items (about 7 minutes), and the full one, with all 165 (about 28 minutes).

How it works

1.

You mark item by item

Not for me, maybe, want to live it, or already lived it. It is not a competition: the list is for knowing yourself, not for scoring.

2.

It stays on your device

The answers are kept in your browser, with no account and no server. Come back to update whenever you like.

3.

As a pair, with no awkwardness

Send the link to your partner. The match shows only what both want; what one declined is shown to nobody.
